# Historical reading editions

The library places Westminster, the Belgic Confession and the Scots Confession under **Confessions of faith**. The first belongs to the Westminster Standards and the second to the Three Forms of Unity. The Westminster Larger Catechism also belongs to the Westminster Standards and appears under **Catechisms**. The Nicene Creed appears under **Historical creeds**. Augustine’s *Confessions*, a spiritual autobiography in the form of a prayer, appears under **Historical writings**.

The project confirmed permission for the supplied Spanish Westminster, Belgic and Scots confessions, the Spanish Augustine edition and the Portuguese Scots edition. The respective credits accompany the text in the reader, on Home excerpts and when sharing. Source hashes identify the precise copies used.

## Westminster

The Spanish text comes from physical pages 32–148 of *Los estándares de Westminster y la forma de gobierno de Westminster* (CLIR, 2010). The selection opens at I.1 and closes at XXXIII.3. Alonzo Ramírez Alvarado translated it with the Seminario Reformado del Caribe. © 2010 Confraternidad Latinoamericana de Iglesias Reformadas. The 579 groups of biblical proof texts retain their Reina Valera 1960 wording and the credit to Sociedades Bíblicas Unidas. Five numbered editorial notes and two notes marked with a dagger are attached to their respective sections. The introductions, catechisms and other works in the volume fall outside this selection. [Facsimile of the supplied edition](https://iglesiacalvary.co/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/confesion-de-fe-de-westminster.pdf)

The English text follows the Edinburgh printing of 1647 in Philip Schaff’s *The Creeds of Christendom*, volume III. Schaff retains the original clauses in XX.4 and XXIII.3 and all five sections of XXXI. The reader includes the English text and all 581 groups of Scripture references. Each group’s call is numbered within its section. [English text and references](https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/creeds3/creeds3.iv.xvii.ii.html) · [CCEL source and rights](https://www.ccel.org/s/schaff/creeds3/cache/creeds3.html3)

The Mexican Spanish edition of 1897 and the Portuguese edition printed in Rio in 1876 follow the American Presbyterian revision, which omits the original XXXI.2 and rewrites the magistrate’s duties in XXIII.3. The bundled editions use the recension in the supplied Spanish PDF. [Arellano facsimile](https://archive.org/details/comentariodelaco00hodg) · [1876 Portuguese facsimile](https://archive.org/details/confissaodefeetc00west_0)

## Westminster Larger Catechism

The Spanish PDF contains all 196 questions and answers. Its metadata credits Marcelo Sánchez as the author of the 2008 file and gives no translator credit. The edition preserves the Spanish questions, answers and printed transition after question 90. Pactantes checked the lettered Scripture references against other editions of the Larger Catechism, including the Philadelphia printing. [Spanish PDF](https://ibe.org.mx/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/catecismo-mayor-de-westminster.pdf) · [Philadelphia printing](https://archive.org/details/constitutionof00pre)

The English text follows the 1860 London printing by T. Nelson and Sons, pp. 127–281. Ian Buchanan’s CC0 transcription supplies all 196 questions and answers. The transition after question 90 comes from the printed edition. The original Scripture proofs are available in the facsimile linked in the reader’s credits. [Printed edition](https://archive.org/details/confessionoffait00westuoft/page/127/mode/1up) · [Versioned transcription](https://github.com/iandbuchanan/confessions-corpus/blob/01ed626ffaabccbe14f3df3ff25c3e7b1b7935d6/json/corpus.json) · [Transcription license](https://github.com/iandbuchanan/confessions-corpus/blob/01ed626ffaabccbe14f3df3ff25c3e7b1b7935d6/LICENSE-TEXTS.md)

The Portuguese text comes from *A confissão de fé e os catecismos da egreja presbyteriana*, Lisbon, Typ. de João Ferreira de Medeiros, 1907, pp. 81–181. The reading follows the historical spelling and includes the Scripture references printed with the questions and answers. The title page names the printer and gives no individual translator. All three editions use the same question numbers, so a reader can change language and resume at the same question. [Portuguese facsimile](https://archive.org/details/confissaodefeeos00west/page/81/mode/1up)

## Nicene Creed

The supplied Spanish sheet from Iglesia Gracia Sobre Gracia (Bautista Reformada) credits *La Biblia de Estudio de La Reforma*, page 2335. Its three paragraphs follow the Western Niceno-Constantinopolitan text, including the procession from the Father and the Son. The reader preserves the asterisk after “universal” and the accompanying editorial note. [Spanish PDF](https://graciasobregracia.org/storage/documents/MDLF21_Recursos_Credo_Niceno.pdf)

The English text comes from the US Episcopal Church’s *Book of Common Prayer*, printed in New York for the General Convention in 1892, pp. 224–225. The Portuguese text comes from the Igreja Lusitana’s *O Livro de Oração Commum*, Porto, 1884, pp. 67–68. Both historical editions profess the creed in the singular. The reader preserves their wording and the Portuguese spelling of the period. [English facsimile](https://archive.org/details/bookcommonpraye00conggoog) · [Portuguese facsimile](https://thefpec.org/prayer-book/scans/pt-BR-001-070.pdf)

## Belgic Confession

The Spanish reading reproduces all 37 articles in the supplied Reformed Church in America PDF, including its biblical citations in the running text. The original is by Guido de Brés (1561). The PDF ends with the RCA permission credit and gives no individual translator. [Spanish edition distributed by the CRCNA](https://www.crcna.org/sites/default/files/belgic_confession-spanish_from_rca.pdf)

The English edition follows the 1840 *Constitution of the Reformed Dutch Church of North America* and includes the closing petition. The reader credits the public-domain print source and the English Wikisource transcription, including its revision and CC BY-SA 4.0 license. [English edition](https://en.wikisource.org/w/index.php?oldid=14414628)

The Portuguese translation for the Reformed Churches of Brazil was prepared by missionaries from the Netherlands and Canada. Ressources chrétiennes credits Monergismo and releases its edition under CC BY-SA 4.0. Its 262 numbered references and the textual notes to articles 9 and 36 are preserved. The reading and share credits separately identify the quotations from *Almeida Revista e Atualizada*, © Sociedade Bíblica do Brasil, 1993. [Portuguese edition](https://www.ressourceschretiennes.com/article/confession-pays-bas-portugais) · [Translation history](https://www.spindleworks.com/library/kroeze/port_intro.htm) · [Bible credit](https://www.spindleworks.com/library/kroeze/belgic_p0rt.htm)

## Scots Confession

The authors of the original are John Knox, John Winram, John Spottiswood, John Willock, John Douglas and John Row. Each language uses the same 25 chapter IDs and includes its own final prayer. The Spanish and Portuguese editions each have 176 numbered biblical references. [Church of Scotland, Book of Confessions](https://www.churchofscotland.org.uk/__data/assets/pdf_file/0007/143584/printBookofConfessions.pdf)

The Spanish text is from physical pages 2–33 of the supplied PDF credited to HombreReformado.org. The PDF names no translator. Several passages share wording with the Spanish *Book of Confessions* published by the Presbyterian Church (U.S.A.). That publication credits the translation committee of Magdalena I. de García, Cecilio Arrastía and Juanleandro Garza, and editor José Luis Velazco M. The numbering and reference apparatus follow the supplied PDF. [Public witness to the Spanish edition](https://www.clir.net/wp-content/uploads/2019/10/escocesa.pdf) · [PC(USA) colophon](https://pcusa.org/sites/default/files/confessions-spanish.pdf#page=6)

The Portuguese reading uses the edition revised by Pr. Franklin Ferreira, published by Luz para o Caminho from Textos da Reforma. The publisher credits Ferreira for revision. The English reading follows Cuthbert Lennox’s historical edition of 1905 and includes its two chapter notes and printed ratification in chapter 25. [Portuguese text](https://www.luz.eti.br/do_confissaoescocesa1560.html) · [English printing](https://catalogue.nli.ie/Record/vtls000450022) · [English transcription](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/48250)

## Augustine

The Spanish edition is the supplied *Confesiones*, translated and annotated by Alfredo Encuentra Ortega, reviewed by Alberto Medina González, © Editorial Gredos, S. A., Madrid, 2010 (ISBN 978-84-249-1505-6). Augustine’s text extends from physical page 111 to page 670. Its 13 books open at the invocation and end with the final Amen. Navigation follows the book, chapter and numbered paragraph. The reader presents a reflowable transcription of Augustine’s main text. The source transcription records the physical page range for each passage. The prefatory study, book summaries and indexes are outside the reading selection. [Bibliographic record](https://catalogo.upsa.es/cgi-bin/koha/opac-detail.pl?biblionumber=205449)

The English text is Edward Bouverie Pusey’s translation (Oxford, 1838), transcribed by Robert S. Munday. Une Sacra’s Portuguese translation from Pusey is included on the agreed noncommercial basis, with source, attribution and CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license. Its 13-source manifest records each book’s URL, revision and digest. Both editions retain their 461 section IDs. [English facsimile](https://archive.org/details/confessionsofsau00auguuoft) · [English transcription](https://gutenberg.pglaf.org/3/2/9/3296/3296-0.txt) · [Portuguese edition](https://unesacra.com/pt/ler/classicos-cristaos/confissoes-de-santo-agostinho/) · [Une Sacra reuse terms](https://unesacra.com/en/help/license-and-citing/)

The alignment file maps the beginning of every Pusey section to its Gredos paragraph and each Gredos paragraph back to the section containing its beginning. The starting words were matched independently against the same Pusey translation numbered by Gerald W. Schlabach. A previously saved English, Portuguese or Spanish reading position opens at the corresponding Gredos paragraph, including where editions divide one passage differently. [Numbered parallel text hosted by Georgetown](https://faculty.georgetown.edu/jod/Englishconfessions.html)

## Storage and verification

`tools/content/historical/editions.json` records each edition’s source, credit, SHA-256 and section counts. The JSON transcriptions supply the ordered groups, questions, texts and apparatus. The SQLite full-text index includes both the question and the answer in separate fields. The importer rebuilds the historical editions, passage aliases and FTS4 index in one transaction. The bundled SQLite database uses schema version 7 and installs as `reformed_documents_v11.db`. Mutable progress, favorites and preferences stay in their existing user stores.

Run `python3 tools/import_historical.py --database assets/db/reformed_documents.db` to rebuild the historical tables. Run `python3 -m unittest discover -s tools -p 'test_import_*.py'` in the importer environment and `flutter test` to verify transcription integrity, indexing, offline installation and reader behavior.
